What the prototype simulates

Each of these says so on screen. None of them should be mistaken for the real thing in a demo.

Login and accountNo account is created; name and email stay in the browser, passwords are never stored.
CheckoutNo payment is taken and no card details are asked for. Confirm simply sets the plan.
Human review"Request" goes to pending and approves itself after a moment, with a reviewer note. The screen says it is simulated.
Endorsing body decisionNever automatic. The presenter clicks a labelled "Simulate the endorsing body's decision" — because the decision is theirs, not Iskra's.
Evidence uploads"Mark as uploaded" records a flag in the case; nothing is transferred.
Advisor replies outside the artifactPre-written walkthrough following the same tier rules; the widget header reads "Scripted demo".
Iskra Services is a trading name of Indigenous Consultants Limited. Iskra is not an authorised endorsing body; readiness views are preparatory, not Home Office scores. Concept prototype — not a live service.
