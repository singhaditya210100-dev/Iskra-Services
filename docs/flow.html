9

The endorsing body decides

An authorised endorsing body (for example UK Endorsing Services, Innovator International, Envestors or GEP) assesses your case on its own. It can approve, ask for more information, or decline. If it approves, it issues your endorsement letter and tells the Home Office. Iskra can answer their questions but has no say in the decision.

What the AI advisor does, and does not do

It does

  • Explain the routes into the UK and what each one needs
  • Ask a few simple questions and remember your answers
  • Recommend a route and say why
  • Explain your assessment and what would improve it
  • Point you to the right specialist or adviser

It does not

  • Give regulated immigration advice. Questions about past refusals, overstays, dependants or the application itself go to an IAA-registered adviser
  • Give scores or verdicts in the chat. Those come from the assessment in your account
  • Promise or influence an endorsement
  • Send anything to an endorsing body without your consent
