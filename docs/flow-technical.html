1 · Six stages, and what opens each gate

Five gates are Iskra's: a free account and three paid plans. The sixth, endorsement, belongs to an authorised endorsing body. Payment unlocks preparation, never the decision.

2 · The full flow, by who acts

Time runs down the page. The founder's decisions form the spine on the left; each stage's work sits in the lane of whoever does it. Everything right of the gold line is outside Iskra.

3 · Every step: purpose, inputs, process, outputs

Inputs are what the step needs to run. Outputs are what it writes to the case, or hands to the next step. Derived values are computed on the fly and never saved.

Pre-login

generic public site, Innovator Founder-centric flow
P1

Land on the site

Founderhomepage

Orient a visitor who does not yet know which route fits, and move them to the advisor. Nine areas, how it works, the four routes, what you get, pricing, about.

Inputs
  • URL. ?reset clears the demo; #route/<key> opens a route page
  • Nothing else is known about the visitor
Process

Static page. After 6 s a nudge offers the advisor and hides itself after 15 s; dismissing it sets a session flag so it does not return.

Outputs
  • None saved to the case
  • sessionStorage.iskra-nudge when dismissed
P2

Route page

FounderROUTES · showRoute()

Explain one route in enough depth to decide whether to check fit: who it is for, what you need, key facts, how Iskra helps, and the official GOV.UK sources.

Inputs
  • Route key: innovator-founder, global-talent, expansion-worker, self-sponsorship
Process

The hash is read on load and on every change; the page renders from the route record and hides the homepage. The back link returns to the routes carousel.

Outputs
  • None saved
  • "Check my fit" opens the advisor with a route-specific opening message

Figures on route pages are indicative and change with GOV.UK guidance; each page says so.

P3

Advisor conversation, visitor tier

AI advisorwidget · tier visitor

Collect the basics a consultant would ask, in four short groups, then recommend a route. In the demo every answer is prefilled so the presenter only presses Send.

Inputs
  • Message text (demo prefill per step)
  • S.turns so far, and S.profile so far
  • Tier line: visitor
  • GROUNDING + context(): routes, tests, bodies, Iskra's plans, the tier rules
Process

Live (artifact): the model returns one JSON envelope {reply, suggestions, gate, stage, profile, regulated}, streamed into the bubble. Elsewhere: VISITOR_SCRIPT, five pre-written steps with the same shape. Groups in order: nationality, location, immigration history → venture, sector, stage, customers → experience, expertise → funding, UK ties. The fifth reply recommends Innovator Founder in two sentences and raises the gate. No scores are ever produced in chat.

Outputs
  • S.turns[] role + content
  • S.profile: nationality, location, immigration, venture, sector, stage, customers, experience, expertise, funding, ukTies
  • S.stage: discovery → profile → pathway
  • S.gated = true
  • S.regulated.triggered if a regulated question stops the advisor

Prototype: outside the artifact the replies are scripted and the widget header says "Scripted demo". Typed text still fills profile gaps, but never overwrites a value already set.

P4

Gate and free account

FoundergateBubble() · auth modal · signIn()

Turn the conversation into an account without losing anything. The gate names the nine areas the free assessment will cover, so the founder knows what they are signing up for.

Inputs
  • Name, email, password (never stored), "keep me logged in"
  • Or Google: email only
  • Existing account: log in
Process

The gate disables the composer and offers "Create free account". Sign-up validates, shows a tick, then enterApp(): first login goes straight to the nine questions.

Outputs
  • user {name, email} in iskra-session (localStorage when remembered, else sessionStorage)
  • S.profile.name from the account name when the chat did not capture one
  • S.gated = false

Prototype: no account exists anywhere; the session lives in the browser.

Free account

intake first, then the assessment, then a dashboard built around one step
A1

Nine questions (intake)

FounderFounder Assessment · intakeForm()

Capture, once, the nine things an endorsing body, an investor or a bank will ask. Every later document is built from these answers.

Inputs
  • Nine free-text answers (demo answers seeded once; editable)
  • S.profile for context
Process

Progress bar counts answered questions. "Skip for now" goes to the dashboard and the dashboard's current step brings the founder back. "Edit answers & re-run" reopens the form later.

Outputs
  • S.intake: problem, solution, market, model, competition, differentiation, founder, funding, uk
  • S.intakeSeen (also set on skip), S.intakeTouched, S.editIntake
A2

Free Founder Assessment

AI advisorrunAssessment() · normaliseAssessment()

Score the case on the nine areas and the three endorsement tests, in plain words, so the founder sees where the evidence is missing before spending anything.

Inputs
  • S.intake, all nine required
  • S.profile
  • Route: Innovator Founder
Process

Live: one model call returns the assessment as JSON, then it is normalised (verdict words, missing fields). Scripted: the worked example. About three seconds either way. Verdicts are strong, moderate or weak.

Outputs
  • S.assessment: route, overall, areas[9] each with key, verdict, summary, assessed, evidence[], gaps[], move, answer; ivs: innovation, viability, scalability each with verdict and note
  • S.assessedLive
Derived, not saved
  • Traffic lights per area and per test
  • Preliminary fit word: weak or moderate count ≤ 3 good, ≤ 6 possible, else needs work
 
  • weakAreas(): every non-strong area, weak first, drives the dashboard, the specialist prompt and the gap analysis
 
  • readinessScore(): mean of strong 100, moderate 55, weak 20; shown only inside paid modules and always labelled preparatory

Boundary: never a Home Office score and never a promise of endorsement; the result panel carries that sentence.

A3

Strengthen your case

Iskra teamstrengthenPanel() · openRequest()

Put the people who close the gaps next to the gaps: UK company formation, eight specialist categories, and an IAA-registered adviser for anything regulated.

Inputs
  • Kind: formation, specialist or regulated
  • Items ticked (at least one)
  • Name, email, phone, message (prefilled with the weak areas)
Process

Modal per kind; validation asks for at least one item; confirmation says who calls back and what they will already know.

Outputs
  • S.requests[] {kind, items, at}

Prototype: nothing is sent. In production the request and the relevant part of the assessment reach Iskra's inbox.

A4

Dashboard and journey map

FoundercurrentStep() · JOURNEY

One thing to do next. The horizontal map across the top shows the seven milestones and their state; the dashboard shows only the current one.

Inputs
  • The whole case S
Process

Seven milestones with done-rules: Profile, Free assessment, Business assessment, Business plan, Gap analysis, Human review, Endorsement (external). The current step is the first thing not done: answer the questions → run the assessment → unlock Business Assessment → unlock Business Plan & Preparation → generate the plan → unlock Endorsement Readiness → request review → submit → endorsed.

Outputs
  • S.module, the last screen opened
  • A locked milestone opens the checkout for the plan it needs
A5

My Profile

FounderPROFILE_GROUPS

One record, captured once, reused at every stage.

Inputs
  • 20 fields in three groups: Personal (5), Founder (5), Business (10)
Process

Fields the conversation filled are marked "from conversation"; anything can be edited by hand.

Outputs
  • S.profile
A6

My plan

Founderavatar menu · module myplan

The one place the plans are laid out side by side: what the account includes today and what each further plan adds.

Inputs
  • S.plan
  • PACKAGES: name, stage, price, what it unlocks
Process

Current plan highlighted; plans already included say so; the rest carry an Unlock button.

Outputs
  • Opens the checkout for the chosen plan

Assess

Business Assessment · £249
B1

Checkout

FounderopenCheckout(key)

Unlock a plan. Styled like Stripe Checkout because that is what founders will meet in production.

Inputs
  • Plan key: readiness, preparation or endorsement
  • Price from PACKAGES, VAT 20 %
  • Email, card number, expiry, CVC, name, country, postcode
Process

Fields are validated and auto-formatted; the Stripe test card is prefilled. Two seconds of processing, then a receipt screen with an "Open" button into the unlocked module.

Outputs
  • S.plan, ranked free 0 → readiness 1 → preparation 2 → endorsement 3; a higher plan includes the lower ones
  • Receipt number ISK- + 8 digits

Prototype: test mode, nothing is charged and nothing leaves the page. What is bought is preparation; the copy says the endorsement decision stays with the endorsing body.

B2

Business Assessment

AI advisormodule business · IVS_MAP, IVS_DETAIL

The same assessment, expanded: what was assessed, the evidence in the answers, the gaps, what would move it, and the founder's own answer, for each of the nine areas; then the three tests, criterion by criterion.

Inputs
  • S.assessment
  • Sixteen criteria mapped to the areas they rest on
Process

Expandable card per area, the first three non-strong ones open. Innovation rests on problem, solution, differentiation; viability on model, founder, funding; scalability on market, UK opportunity, competition.

Outputs
  • Nothing new saved
  • PDF via pdfAssessment() (jsPDF), share via Web Share or a copied summary and link

Prepare

Business Plan & Preparation · £749
C1

Business plan

AI advisormodule plan · generatePlan()

Seventeen sections written from the assessment, each tied to the area it rests on, so the founder can see which sections are strong and which need evidence before a human ever reads it.

Inputs
  • S.assessment, S.intake, S.profile
  • PLAN_SECTIONS: 17 keys, each with the areas it is based on
Process

Live: one call returns all sections; where the answers do not support a section it says so rather than inventing. Scripted: composePlan() writes each section from the intake answers. Each section's status is the weakest area it rests on: Strong or Needs evidence. "Based on" chips jump to that assessment card. Regenerate bumps the version.

Outputs
  • S.bplan {sections{17}, live, version}
  • S.bplanEdits {key: text} for hand edits
  • PDF via pdfPlan()
C2

Gap analysis

AI advisormodule gaps

What must improve before human review, in priority order, with the action for each.

Inputs
  • S.assessment: non-strong areas, weak first
  • Each area's "what would move it"
  • readinessScore(), S.evidence
Process

Top gaps with a recommended action each; a 30 / 60 / 90-day remediation plan; evidence completeness as the share of the ten evidence items on file.

Outputs
  • All derived; nothing new saved
C3

Documents and evidence

Foundermodule evidence · EVIDENCE

Every claim in the plan needs something behind it. Ten items, each mapped to the test it supports.

Inputs
  • Ten items: letters of intent, paid pilot invoice, prototype demonstration, founder CV, financial model, market sizing, competitor analysis, expansion plan, English certificate, maintenance funds
  • Each tagged Viability, Innovation, Founder or Scalability
Process

"Mark as uploaded" toggles an item; the count feeds the gap analysis and the human review.

Outputs
  • S.evidence {key: true}

Prototype: nothing is uploaded; marking records it in the case only.

Review and endorsement

Endorsement Readiness · £1,490, then the external decision
D1

Human review

Iskra teammodule review

A qualified consultant reads the whole case before it goes anywhere near an endorsing body.

Inputs
  • S.profile, S.assessment, S.bplan, S.evidence, the gap list
Process

"Request human review" sets the case to pending; the consultant approves it for referral or returns it with notes. The five-row tracker on the right shows preparation, human review, authorised body, decision, letter.

Outputs
  • S.human.status: none → pending → approved
  • Reviewer note shown on approval

Prototype: approval arrives by itself after 2.6 s and the screen says it is simulated.

D2

Endorsing body

ExternalBODIES · #submit-body · #sim-decide

Hand the prepared case to the body the founder chooses. From here on, Iskra answers information requests and has no part in the decision.

Inputs
  • S.external.body: UK Endorsing Services, Innovator International, Envestors or GEP
  • Explicit consent to share the referral pack: profile, plan, evidence map, assessment, gap analysis, version history
  • £1,000 + VAT paid by the founder to the body, outside the platform
Process

Submit → "submitted", then "pending" once the body has it. Real outcomes: approve, further information, decline. Approval issues the official endorsement letter and the body notifies the Home Office.

Outputs
  • S.external.status: none → submitted → pending → approved
  • Journey milestone 7 becomes Done

Prototype: the decision is never automatic. The presenter clicks a labelled "Simulate the endorsing body's decision", because the decision is the body's and not Iskra's.

E1

After endorsement

Iskra teamdashboard current step · review module

The letter is issued; the visa application is regulated work and the company needs to exist. Both are handed to people.

Inputs
  • S.external.status = approved
Process

The dashboard's current step becomes "Endorsed. Next, the visa application" with two calls to action: an IAA-registered adviser for the application, and UK company formation with accounting, tax, payroll and compliance behind it.

Outputs
  • Requests into S.requests
  • The Home Office decides the visa, outside the platform
X1

Demo controls

Presenterwidget ↻ · footer Reset demo · ?reset

Start clean between demos without losing the parts worth keeping.

Inputs
  • Which reset: conversation or everything
Process

↻ in the widget keeps the plan and the intake, clears the chat and the assessment. Footer "Reset demo" (press twice) clears the case and the session. ?reset or ?demo on the URL clears on load.

Outputs
  • S back to blank(), user cleared

4 · The assessment engine and everything that reads it

One record, produced once from the nine answers, is the source for every later screen and document. Changing an answer and re-running changes all of them.

5 · What the advisor may do, by tier

The same widget behaves differently depending on who is talking to it. The tier is sent to the model on every turn, and the page enforces it too.

8 · What the prototype simulates

Each of these says so on screen. None should be mistaken for the real thing in a demo.

Login and accountNo account is created; name and email stay in the browser, passwords are never stored.
CheckoutStripe-style test mode with the 4242 card prefilled. Nothing is charged; Pay sets the plan and shows a receipt.
Assessment and plan outside the artifactA worked example and a composed plan stand in for the model; the panels say "scripted".
Human review"Request" goes to pending and approves itself after a moment, with a reviewer note.
Endorsing body decisionNever automatic. The presenter clicks a labelled "Simulate the endorsing body's decision".
Evidence uploads and requests"Mark as uploaded" records a flag; specialist and formation requests are recorded locally and nothing is sent.
