What you get

Not a document. A stronger case.

Assessment, plan and review from the AI and Iskra's team, then the people who make a UK company real.

Free Founder Assessment

Nine areas, scored strong, moderate or needs work, with what to fix first. Run it again as your case improves.

Business Assessment

Every area expanded: what was assessed, the evidence in your answers, the gaps, and what would move it. Plus the three endorsement tests, criterion by criterion.

AI-written Business Plan

Seventeen sections written from your assessment, each linked to the area it rests on, so you can see which are strong and which need evidence.

Gap analysis & evidence map

The top gaps in priority order, a 30 / 60 / 90-day plan, and every claim mapped to the evidence behind it.

Human review & referral pack

A qualified Iskra consultant reviews the whole case and packages it for the authorised endorsing body you choose.

Authorised endorsing body

UK Endorsing Services, Innovator International, Envestors or GEP assess your case independently and decide. Iskra prepares; they endorse.

UK company formation & registrations

Companies House incorporation, registered office, VAT, PAYE and business banking, set up properly from day one.
